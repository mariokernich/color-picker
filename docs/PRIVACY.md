# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Color Picker does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

- **Screen contents**: when you start the picker, the app captures your displays so it can show the
  loupe and read the color under it. Captures are kept in memory only while the picker is open and are
  never saved or sent anywhere.
- **Your colors and settings**: history, palettes and preferences are stored locally on your device
  in the app's data folder. They are not uploaded.
- **Purchases**: subscriptions are handled by Apple (Mac App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store).
  The app only receives the information it needs to unlock features. Please refer to Apple's and
  Microsoft's privacy policies for how they process payment data.
- **Analytics and tracking**: the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
