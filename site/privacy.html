2. Die App „ChipDeck“

Die App erhebt, übermittelt und verkauft keine personenbezogenen Daten.

  • Bildschirminhalte: Beim Aufnehmen einer Farbe erfasst die App den Bildschirm, um die Lupe anzuzeigen und die Farbe unter dem Mauszeiger auszulesen. The ChipDeck app

The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

  • Screen contents: while you pick a color, the app captures your displays to show the loupe and read the color under the cursor. Captures are kept in memory only while the picker is open and are never saved or sent anywhere. On macOS this requires the Screen Recording permission, which you can revoke in System Settings at any time.
  • Colors and settings are stored locally on your device only.
  • No network access: the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking and does not connect to any server.
